# Preventive Maintenance Schedule

This document is part of the fictional Kestrel Motors Plant 4 operations handbook, written as sample data for RAGGuard.

## Daily tasks

Operators check air pressure gauges before production; pneumatic tools need between 6 and 7 bar. Operators also empty the compressed air filter drain at the end of each shift.

## Weekly tasks

Maintenance technicians lubricate conveyor chains every Monday with food-grade chain oil. Technicians also inspect all emergency stop buttons weekly by pressing each one and confirming the line stops.

## Monthly tasks

The robot cell safety light curtains are tested monthly with the test rod supplied by the manufacturer. Hydraulic press oil level and hose condition are checked every month.

## Spare parts

Critical spare parts, such as the vision camera, the PLC power supply and conveyor drive belts, are kept in the line-side spares cabinet. The minimum stock for each critical spare part is two units.

## Maintenance records

All completed maintenance work is logged in the computerized maintenance management system before the end of the shift in which it was done.
