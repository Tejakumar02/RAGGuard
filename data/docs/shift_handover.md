# Shift Handover

This document is part of the fictional Kestrel Motors Plant 4 operations handbook, written as sample data for RAGGuard.

## Shift times

Plant 4 runs three shifts. The first shift runs from 06:00 to 14:00, the second shift from 14:00 to 22:00, and the third shift from 22:00 to 06:00.

## Handover meeting

Outgoing and incoming team leaders hold a 10 minute handover meeting at the line-side board at the start of each shift. Both team leaders sign the handover sheet when the meeting ends.

## What to report

The handover must cover open safety issues, units in quarantine, equipment running in degraded mode, and output against the shift target. Any piece of equipment that is locked out must be reported by name together with the person who holds the lock.

## Production targets

The shift target for the tower assembly line is 60 units. If the line finishes the shift more than 10 percent below target, the outgoing team leader writes a short explanation on the handover sheet.
