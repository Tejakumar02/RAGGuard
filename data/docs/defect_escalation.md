# Defect Handling and Escalation

This document is part of the fictional Kestrel Motors Plant 4 operations handbook, written as sample data for RAGGuard.

## Severity levels

Defects are classified into three severity levels. A critical defect affects safety or braking function. A major defect affects fit or performance but not safety. A minor defect is cosmetic only.

## Response times

A critical defect stops the line immediately, and the quality engineer must respond within 15 minutes. A major defect must be reviewed by the team leader before the end of the current shift. A minor defect is logged and reviewed at the weekly quality meeting.

## Quarantine

Any unit with a critical or major defect is moved to the red quarantine rack and labelled with a hold tag. Units on the quarantine rack may only be released by the quality engineer.

## Recording defects

Every defect is recorded in the quality log with the unit serial number, defect code, time and operator ID. Defects found by the vision inspection station are recorded automatically with a saved image of the failed frame.

## Repeat defects

If the same defect code appears three times within one shift, the team leader must open a root cause investigation using the 5 Whys method.
