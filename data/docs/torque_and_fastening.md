# Torque and Fastening Standards

This document is part of the fictional Kestrel Motors Plant 4 operations handbook, written as sample data for RAGGuard.

## Torque values

End cover bolts are tightened to 45 Nm. Drain plugs are tightened to 30 Nm. Cut-off valve mounting screws are tightened to 18 Nm. All torque values have a tolerance of plus or minus 5 percent.

## Tightening sequence

End cover bolts must be tightened in a cross pattern so the cover seats evenly. Each bolt is first run down to 50 percent of the final torque, then taken to full torque in a second pass.

## Tool calibration

Every electric torque wrench is calibrated every 90 days by the metrology lab. A wrench whose calibration sticker has expired must be removed from the line and tagged as out of service.

## Thread locker

A drop of blue medium-strength thread locker is applied to drain plug threads before installation. Thread locker is not used on end cover bolts.

## Verification

The vision inspection station confirms that the drill tool visited every required fastening position. A missing tightening step stops the cycle and turns the tower lamp red.
